## 🎨 ART DIRECTION GUIDE

### COLOR PALETTE
**Primary Colors:**
- **Saffron Gold** (#F5A623) — New beginnings, optimism, transformation
- **Blood Orange** (#E74C3C) — Fire, passion, destruction
- **Ember Red** (#C0392B) — Deep transformation, phoenix fire
- **Charcoal Black** (#2C3E50) — Old self, decay, necessary darkness
- **Ash Gray** (#95A5A6) — Transition state, renewal in progress
- **Deep Violet** (#6C3483) — Magic, alchemy, spiritual rebirth

**Accent Colors:**
- Bone white for highlights
- Forest green for new growth elements
- Deep indigo for shadow/depth

### VISUAL LANGUAGE
- **Split compositions**: Each card shows decay/old on one side, growth/new on the other
- **Phoenix imagery**: Reimagined for each card (not literal—use phoenix energy/fire/metamorphosis)
- **Decomposition elements**: Beautiful, never grotesque (rotting leaves, peeling bark, ash)
- **Figures in transition**: Humans shown mid-transformation, ambiguous between old and new
- **Organic patterns**: Burned landscapes regenerating, vines through ruins, roots breaking concrete
- **Dynamic composition**: Movement, wind, fire, water—never static
- **Texture**: Charred edges, smoky wisps, ash particles, growth breaking through

### COMPOSITION RULES
1. **Always show duality**: Before/after, ending/beginning, destruction/creation
2. **Figures are inclusive**: Various races, body types, abilities, genders visible
3. **Nature as protagonist**: Plants, fire, water, stone play active roles
4. **Never toxic positivity**: Show the real struggle, not just the happy ending
5. **Embers as visual motif**: Ash particles visible in most cards as transition element
6. **Asymmetrical balance**: Unequal sides emphasize transformation, not stasis
